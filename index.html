Does the template include a working SaaS backend?

No. This download is a front-end website template. Authentication, database, billing, client portals, email delivery, and integrations require your own application and services.

Can I connect my own signup or contact service?

Yes. Connect the buttons and lead form to your application, form processor, or CRM. The included sample form only demonstrates the interface and does not transmit or store submissions.

Yes. Edit the displayed plan details and the monthly/annual values in the HTML. The billing switch is interactive, but payment processing and subscription management are not included.
